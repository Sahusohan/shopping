About TinyBotics

Our Mission

At TinyBotics, we believe that advanced AI should be accessible to everyone — not just tech enthusiasts. Our mission is to create compact, affordable AI companions that seamlessly integrate into daily life, helping people stay organized, focused, and connected.

Why TinyBot Pro?

TinyBot Pro is our flagship product — a palm-sized AI robot powered by GPT-4 that combines voice control, emotional intelligence, and real-time assistance. Whether you need help with tasks, want to stay on top of your schedule, or simply enjoy having a smart companion, TinyBot Pro is designed for you.
